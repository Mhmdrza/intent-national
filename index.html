فریم روانی:

اثر مورد انتظار:

سیاست با مراد ویسی: شمشیر ترامپ بر سر خامنه‌ای

🎯 نیت پنهان:

To conduct psychological warfare by demoralizing regime supporters through fear of renewed 'Maximum Pressure' or military action, while simultaneously energizing the opposition by portraying the regime's collapse as inevitable due to external forces.

💡 پاتک رسانه‌ای:

Emphasize the failure of previous 'Maximum Pressure' campaigns to topple the system; characterize the narrative as foreign-funded fear-mongering (PsyOps); highlight national resilience and independence from U.S. domestic politics; and project a stance of indifference to who holds office in the White House.

مشاهده جزئیات فریم‌بندی

فریم روانی: Existential Threat / 'Sword of Damocles'. The narrative uses high-stakes conflict imagery, personalizing the geopolitical tension as a direct duel between Trump (the executioner/aggressor) and Khamenei (the vulnerable target), implying pending doom.

اثر مورد انتظار: Heightened public anxiety within Iran regarding war and economic instability; erosion of confidence among the regime's rank-and-file supporters; and consolidation of the anti-regime narrative within the diaspora.

شمخانی: توانمندی‌های موشکی ایران قابل مذاکره نیست

🎯 نیت پنهان:

To preemptively shut down Western attempts to expand the scope of nuclear talks (like the JCPOA) to include defense issues, and to signal resilience to domestic hardliners and regional adversaries.

💡 پاتک رسانه‌ای:

Shift the focus to 'Regional Stability' and 'Economic Cost'. Argue that the missile program fuels a dangerous arms race that isolates Iran, and that stubbornness on this issue perpetuates economic sanctions that harm the Iranian public.

مشاهده جزئیات فریم‌بندی

فریم روانی: Deterrence and Sovereignty. The statement uses a tone of unyielding authority to frame military capability not as a threat, but as a non-negotiable right to self-defense.

اثر مورد انتظار: Solidifies Iran's negotiating stance by stripping leverage from opponents regarding military concessions; likely provokes condemnation from the US and EU and heightens regional security anxieties.

ترامپ پس از دیدار با نتانیاهو: اصرار کردم مذاکرات با ایران ادامه یابد

🎯 نیت پنهان:

To mitigate immediate market panic and public fear of war in the region; to signal to Tehran that a new deal (on Trump's terms) is the goal rather than regime change; to soften the perceived alignment between US and Israeli hawkishness.

💡 پاتک رسانه‌ای:

Characterize the statement as a 'Good Cop/Bad Cop' tactic with Israel; highlight that Trump's definition of 'negotiation' historically involves coercive economic pressure; recall the 2018 JCPOA withdrawal to undermine trust in his diplomatic overtures.

مشاهده جزئیات فریم‌بندی

فریم روانی: Pragmatic diplomacy; positioning Trump as a 'Deal-Maker' rather than a pure warmonger; asserting US autonomy over Israeli foreign policy preferences.

اثر مورد انتظار: Reduction of geopolitical risk premiums in Iranian markets; confusion among political analysts expecting 'Maximum Pressure 2.0'; creation of diplomatic ambiguity that buys time.

سناتور کندی به ایران‌اینترنشنال: ترامپ به زودی از مذاکره با تهران ناامید می‌شود

🎯 نیت پنهان:

To manage the expectations of the Iranian opposition diaspora (the outlet's primary audience) by reassuring them that the US will not normalize relations with Tehran; to exert psychological pressure on the Iranian regime by signaling that sanctions relief is unlikely; to lobby against potential future diplomatic engagement.

💡 پاتک رسانه‌ای:

Highlight Trump's history of unconventional diplomacy and transactional nature (e.g., North Korea) to suggest he ignores establishment hawkish advice; emphasize that legislative opinions (Senators) do not dictate executive foreign policy; argue that abandoning diplomacy increases the risk of military conflict, which is unpopular.

مشاهده جزئیات فریم‌بندی

فریم روانی: Deterministic pessimism regarding diplomacy; portraying the Iranian regime as an immutable bad-faith actor; framing a return to 'Maximum Pressure' policies as the only logical conclusion for a Trump administration.

اثر مورد انتظار: Strengthens hardline anti-regime sentiment among the audience; diminishes hope among Iranians for economic relief through negotiations; reinforces the narrative that the current Iranian system is incompatible with international diplomacy.

مداخله بین‌المللی و سازماندهی سیاسی برای عبور از جمهوری اسلامی

🎯 نیت پنهان:

To normalize the concept of foreign interference—often a taboo subject—by presenting it as a necessary component of liberation. It also aims to position specific diaspora opposition groups as the legitimate interlocutors for foreign powers, thereby soliciting Western backing and resources.

💡 پاتک رسانه‌ای:

Highlight the historical failures and instability resulting from foreign interventions in the region (e.g., Libya, Afghanistan, Iraq). Frame the proponents of this view as 'dependent' on foreign powers rather than relying on the national will, appealing to Iranian nationalism and sovereignty. Question the legitimacy and representativeness of the 'political organizers' living abroad.

مشاهده جزئیات فریم‌بندی

فریم روانی: Strategic necessity/Pragmatism. The narrative frames regime change not just as a desire but as a logistical project requiring external leverage ('intervention') and internal discipline ('organization'), implicitly suggesting that organic, leaderless protests are insufficient.

اثر مورد انتظار: Among opposition supporters, it is likely to generate hope and urgency for coalition-building. Among the general public, it may create division regarding the definition and safety of 'intervention' (e.g., sanctions vs. military). For the regime, it serves to confirm narratives of a 'foreign conspiracy,' potentially justifying harsher crackdowns on grounds of national security.
